<p class="cs-kicker">Small business owners knew their goal, like getting more leads. They didn't know which tool would get them there. So we stopped leading with tools.</p>

## The challenge

Infusionsoft's marketplace connected small business owners with apps, integrations and prebuilt marketing campaigns created by partners and internal teams. The first version put tools at the forefront, so owners had to already know what they were looking for.

<ul class="stats-row cs-stats cs-wide">
  <li><strong>300+</strong><span>Integrations and ready-made campaigns to search</span></li>
  <li><strong>2–4 wks</strong><span>Of support calls to launch a first campaign, if owners launched one at all</span></li>
  <li><strong>10</strong><span>Small business owners interviewed</span></li>
  <li><strong>7</strong><span>Usability test participants</span></li>
</ul>

## My role

> **My role:** Design lead. I owned research, UX and UI from discovery to launch.
>
> **Team:** 4 people: me, a product manager, a front-end developer and an engineer.

## Research

<div class="cs-goals cs-wide cs-three">
  <div><p class="eyebrow">Interviews</p><p><strong>10 owners</strong>, to shape the first concept</p></div>
  <div><p class="eyebrow">Usability testing</p><p><strong>7 participants</strong>, to choose the final direction</p></div>
  <div><p class="eyebrow">Evaluation</p><p><strong>Quantitative and qualitative data</strong>, to measure what worked</p></div>
</div>

<blockquote class="cs-quote"><p>Leading with tools slowed people down. Owners think in goals, like getting more leads or getting paid faster, not in software categories.</p><cite>What user testing showed</cite></blockquote>

<div class="cs-learn cs-wide cs-four-up">
  <div><span>01</span><strong>The value wasn't clear.</strong><p>Owners couldn't see how a tool would help their business.</p></div>
  <div><span>02</span><strong>The purpose wasn't clear.</strong><p>Tool names and descriptions didn't explain what each tool did.</p></div>
  <div><span>03</span><strong>No idea what to search for.</strong><p>Without guidance, owners couldn't find solutions.</p></div>
  <div><span>04</span><strong>Listings were inconsistent.</strong><p>Partner and support teams needed help writing clear listings.</p></div>
</div>

## Insights to decisions

<div class="cs-table cs-wide">

| What we learned | What we changed |
| --- | --- |
| People shop by goal, not by tool | Rebuilt the homepage and navigation around five goals: attract leads, convert, make money, follow up and generate referrals |
| The value and purpose weren't clear | Each goal explains how it helps before any tool is named. Listing cards lead with what the tool does, then price and ratings |
| Owners didn't know what to search for | Search moved to the top with goal prompts like “Follow up with leads”, plus Featured, Most Popular and Newest lists as starting points |
| Listings were inconsistent | A templated submission form for partners, and a QA review for clarity before each listing went live |

</div>

## Outcome

A goal-focused marketplace with plain-language search and goal categories. Every listing was tagged to a goal, so owners could browse by what they wanted to achieve.

<ul class="stats-row cs-stats cs-wide">
  <li><strong>&lt;1 week</strong><span>To launch a first campaign, down from 2–4 weeks</span></li>
  <li><strong>~50%</strong><span>Estimated increase in owners using marketplace tools</span></li>
  <li><strong>2x</strong><span>Support teams recommending marketplace tools</span></li>
</ul>

Support calls dropped too, because owners could find and set up solutions on their own.

## Reflection

<blockquote class="cs-quote"><p>Users didn't want tools. They wanted outcomes.</p><cite>When we designed around their goals instead of our catalog, owners found the right solution and launched in days instead of weeks.</cite></blockquote>
